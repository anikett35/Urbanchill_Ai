CHAPTER 1
INTRODUCTION & PROJECT OVERVIEW
1.1 Project Title
UrbanChill AI: A Geo-Intelligent Digital Twin Platform for Urban Heat Resilience

1.1 Proposed Solution
UrbanChill AI proposes a comprehensive Geo-Intelligent Digital Twin Platform capable of monitoring, analyzing, and predicting urban heat conditions.
The platform combines satellite imagery, GIS technologies, spatial databases, and machine learning into a single interactive application.
The workflow begins with a realistic 3D Earth visualization where users can explore the globe and select any city. Once a city is selected, the platform retrieves satellite imagery, calculates Land Surface Temperature (LST) and NDVI, performs spatial analysis, predicts heat-risk levels using machine learning, and presents results through an interactive planning dashboard.
The system further enables planners to simulate urban cooling strategies, compare environmental indicators, and generate analytical reports for informed decision-making.
The proposed platform shifts urban heat management from reactive observation toward proactive planning.
